Branch 02 · long-term exposure definition

Environment turns metocean data into fatigue-ready representative load cases.

This branch takes raw environmental information and converts it into a structured fatigue environment: long-term scatter, directionality, regular or irregular representation, exposure weighting and the final load-case matrix for global simulations.

What this branch must deliver

  • A traceable environmental basis used for fatigue assessment.
  • Representative regular-wave classes or irregular sea states.
  • Exposure values and environmental load-case IDs ready for downstream global analysis.
Key principle: the environment branch is not just data storage. It is the logic that preserves long-term occurrence while reducing the environmental space to a practical yet technically credible fatigue case set.
