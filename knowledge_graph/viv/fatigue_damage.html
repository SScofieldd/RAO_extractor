Study-grounded VIV / SHEAR7 branch

This branch completes the source-supported Stage IV chain without inventing the detailed fatigue equations that are not present in the uploaded VIV modules.

The supplied modules state that SHEAR7 Stage IV combines response cases and calculates RMS stress, fatigue damage rate, fatigue life and drag amplification. They do not provide the detailed Section 7 fatigue equations, so this page preserves that boundary rather than inventing a damage formula.

Memory: Metrics means response statistics → stress → fatigue outputs. The exact fatigue algorithm must come from the actual SHEAR7 Section 7 / project procedure.

What the supplied modules support

  • Fatigue life is a Stage IV output

  • Drag amplification is another Stage IV metric
