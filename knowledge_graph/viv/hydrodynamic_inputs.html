Study-grounded VIV / SHEAR7 branch

Hydrodynamic inputs connect wake physics to the reduced-order SHEAR7 response model.

This branch separates the mechanics-based force forms from the empirical/calibrated hydrodynamic closures used by SHEAR7. It shows what must be consistent before Stage III response iteration.

Memory: Mass → Lift → Loss are the hydrodynamic building blocks used in Stage III before the amplitude iteration closes.

What this branch must establish

  • Hydrodynamic diameter and fluid properties
  • Added-mass basis compatible with modal analysis
  • Lift-coefficient basis
  • Hydrodynamic + structural damping treatment
  • Clear theory-based vs empirical boundaries
