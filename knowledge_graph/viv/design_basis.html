Minimum design-basis checklist

Freeze these items before the VIV current-case definition and solver setup are treated as production analysis.

  • VIV assessment objective and scope are explicit.
  • Lifecycle/configuration conditions are identified.
  • Structural model source and revision are traceable.
  • Current/environment source and direction convention are documented.
  • Hydrodynamic diameter, damping and suppression-device treatment are defined.
  • Solver/method and key model options are frozen.
  • S-N curve, fatigue factor/DFF and acceptance criteria are stated.
  • Case naming links each run to configuration, current case and result set.
Typical high-level chain: Design Basis → Current Cases → Structural / Modal Model → VIV Response → Stress → Fatigue Damage → Acceptance.
