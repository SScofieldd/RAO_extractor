Study-grounded VIV / SHEAR7 branch

The VIV stress branch makes the central fatigue connection explicit: displacement controls motion, but curvature controls bending strain and stress.

This page follows the exact source-supported bridge from displacement to curvature, bending strain/stress and RMS stress.

Memory: Displacement shows motion; curvature drives bending stress. Higher modes can be fatigue-critical even when displacement looks modest.

Stress branch outputs

  • Curvature distribution
  • Case-specific bending stress contributions
  • RMS stress metric
  • Spanwise governing locations
  • Clear boundary between source-supported beam stress and project-specific local stress recovery
