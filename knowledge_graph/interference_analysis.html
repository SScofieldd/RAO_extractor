Sensitivity Analysis — What Is Driving the Result?

Sensitivity is used to identify the physical driver, not merely to create another result table.

Departure Angle Sensitivity

Vary the departure geometry and re-run the governing / critical cases.

Question: Can altering the departure geometry move the umbilical away from the tendons?

Armour Sensitivity

Modify line stiffness / weight characteristics consistently with the candidate armour change.

Question: Is excessive line movement or dynamic instability contributing to the interference?

Offset Sensitivity

Vary vessel offset / setdown boundary conditions around the governing configuration.

Question: Is the vessel boundary condition itself the main driver?
Project interpretation supplied: vessel offset / setdown appears particularly important, while increased armour also offers a strong design improvement. The page treats these as project-study conclusions, not universal behaviour.

Minimum QA set before issuing a conclusion

  • Confirm geometry, line identifiers and topside / subsea positions.
  • Check line segmentation and contact / clearance resolution near critical zones.
  • Verify vessel offset / setdown sign conventions and headings.
  • Confirm current profile and direction convention.
  • Document wake basis and VIV drag amplification assumptions.
  • For dynamics, verify time step, duration, transient handling and associated waves.
  • Plot clearance versus arc length and time, not only a single minimum value.
  • Record governing time, arc length, pair, heading and offset.
  • Where collision is accepted, retain impact / clash metrics and local structural check evidence.
  • Keep sensitivity results tied back to the identified engineering driver.
