Mission objective

Deliver the control link.

Install an electro-hydraulic umbilical from the lay vessel to the subsea distribution unit (SDU).

Balance vessel speed and umbilical payout. Too little payout raises tension; too much can reduce local bend radius.

Engineering insight

Payout versus tension

Payout too low for the vessel's movement can increase line tension. Too much slack can create an unfavorable touchdown shape.

📖 Open engineering handbook

Umbilicals deliver electrical power, communications, hydraulic services and sometimes chemicals for subsea control; they are not normally the main produced-oil conduit.

Tension and bend radius are different constraints. Excessive tension or curvature can damage internal elements. Real installation involves catenary response, contact, motions, fatigue, installation configuration and vendor-specific limits.

AHC represents simplified active heave compensation, reducing wave-driven tension fluctuations in this game. Actual capability depends on the specific system.
